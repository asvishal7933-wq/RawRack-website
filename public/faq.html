FAQ.

HOW DOES CUSTOMIZATION WORK?

Select a product, choose size and colour, upload your PNG/JPG/WEBP design and leave a placement note.

WHAT FILE SHOULD I UPLOAD?

PNG is preferred for graphics with transparent backgrounds. JPG and WEBP are also supported. Current upload limit: 8MB.

WHERE DO YOU SHIP?

Bangalore only at launch. We can expand locations later.

HOW DO I PAY?

Checkout is designed for Razorpay, supporting available UPI, cards and net-banking methods on the customer's device.

HOW DO I CONTACT YOU?

WhatsApp: 7022769412.

CAN I CHANGE MY ORDER?

Contact RawRack as quickly as possible after ordering. Custom production can limit changes once the design enters production.
